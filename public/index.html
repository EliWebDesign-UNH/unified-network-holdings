About the LLC

Unified Network Holdings, LLC is a New Mexico limited liability company formed in 2024. The company exists as a single, family-owned holding structure — no outside investors, no fund life, no exit clock. Capital comes from the company itself; capital goes back into the company itself.

The portfolio is small by design. We add a subsidiary when the operating model is clear, the customer is identified, and the work can be sustained long-term. We do not chase growth for its own sake.

Day-to-day operations are run by a single operator. The holding company exists to give the operating subsidiaries governance, capital, and a long horizon — nothing else.

Values

Three principles, applied consistently.

  • i.

    Long horizons

    We hold, we compound, we improve. The companies in the portfolio are built to outlast the founder's attention.

  • ii.

    Bootstrapped, always

    No outside capital, no investors to please. Every dollar in the company was earned by the company.

  • iii.

    Operator-led

    The same person who decides the strategy answers the support email. The holding company is small on purpose.

Statement

Unified Network Holdings was founded on a simple premise: that small operating companies, given patient capital and long horizons, can serve their communities better than venture-backed scale-ups chasing growth for its own sake. The portfolio exists to prove that premise — one operating company at a time, in the New Mexico market we know well.

— Eli Arenas, Founder
