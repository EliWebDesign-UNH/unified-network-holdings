§ 01

Mandate

A holding company exists to do three things well: own, govern, and steward. We do those three things for our operating subsidiaries — nothing else.

i.

Governance

Corporate structure, filings, registered agent, banking, accounting, legal posture. The plumbing that lets an operating company focus on its customers instead of its paperwork.

ii.

Capital

Patient, permanent capital. No fund life, no LP pressure, no exit clock. We invest what we can afford to lose, and we don't borrow against the portfolio.

iii.

Stewardship

Long-term thinking, defensible positioning, and the operating infrastructure each subsidiary needs to grow without being distracted by overhead.

§ 02

Approach

Four principles guide every decision at the holding level. The framework is simple. The discipline is staying inside it.

i.

Long horizons

We're building for decades, not quarters. A holding company that needs to "exit" its own subsidiaries isn't a holding company — it's a fund in disguise. We hold, we compound, we improve.

ii.

Bootstrapped, always

No outside capital, no investors to please, no board to report to. Every dollar in the company was earned by the company. That freedom is the point.

iii.

Operator-led

The same person who decides which subsidiary to launch is the same person who answers the support email. No layers between strategy and execution. The holding company is small on purpose.

iv.

Aligned interests

Family capital, family governance, family reputation on the line. When our subsidiaries win, we win. When they have a problem, it's our problem too — not a quarterly call away.
